SAINT AUGUSTINE OF HIPPO

SAINT AUGUSTINE OF HIPPO (Augustinus Hipponensis /au̯.ɡusˈtiː.nus hipːoˈnɛnsis/) is commonly called the Doctor of Grace (Doctor Gratiae) by the Catholic Church and many other theologians and philosophers for his works on theology and philosophy.

Because his works are widely available in the English language, I will be using the translation used by the Catholic website 'New Advent' as well as what translations I can find that are free to use and without copyright restraints (such as copies that might be found on archive.org).
